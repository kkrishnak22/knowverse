Robot nuvvu cheppindi matrame, nuvvu cheppina order lone chesindi. Computer kuda ade vidhanga pani chestundi. Nee steps list ni [[What is a program?|program]] antaru.

Steps order kuda mukhyam. Ee steps tea chestayi, kaani kalisipoyayi. Vaatini order lo pettu.

```kv-tea
```

Kitchen robot nee steps ni eppudu fix cheyyaledu. Nuvvu cheppithe khaali kettle ni kuda marigistundi. Computer kuda ante: nee steps correct ayina, tappu ayina, vaatine follow avutundi.

```kv-quiz
? Robot wall ki guddukundi. Enduku?
- Adi confuse ayyindi
+ Daaniki ichina step ne adi exact ga chesindi
- Adi aa vaipu vellaalanukundi
> Robot emi decide cheyyadu. Step tappu ayina, nuvvu raasina step ne chestundi.

? Robot nee steps ni ae order lo chesindi?
+ Modati step nunchi chivari step varaku, okkokkati ga
- Anni okesari
- Daaniki nachina order lo
> Okka step, tarvata next di. Prathi program ilaage run avutundi.

? Kettle ni marigiche mundu poyyamani cheppav. Robot em chesindi?
- Mundu neellu marigindi, endukante adi artham untundi
+ Cheppinatte challani neellu poyyindi
- Aagi ninnu adigindi
> Edi artham ayyedo computer ki teliyadu. Nuvvu ichina order matrame daaniki telusu.

? Programming ante enti?
- Computer ki em kaavalo guess cheyyadam
+ Computer ki em cheyyalo exact ga, okka step tarvata okati cheppadam
- Computer ni tayaru cheyyadam
> Nuvvu exact steps raastav. Computer vaatini follow avutundi.
```
